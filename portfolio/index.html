Process / How I work

From brief
to final.

Each project starts by understanding what needs to communicate, then the visual system is built, refined and prepared for the formats it actually needs.

01

Understand

Clarify the audience, message, format and what the work needs to achieve.

02

Build direction

Set hierarchy, typography, composition, visual language and motion direction where needed.

03

Refine

Remove weak choices, improve consistency and test the design across its real formats.

04

Deliver

Prepare final artwork, responsive assets or motion outputs cleanly for handoff and publishing.
